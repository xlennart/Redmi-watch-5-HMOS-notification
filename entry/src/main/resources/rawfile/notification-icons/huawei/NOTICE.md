# Huawei brand artwork

HuaweiWallet.png is the official wallet logo published by Huawei Consumer Support:
https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/support/content/zh-cn16013292/

HuaweiPay.png is the unmodified 1x PNG in the Huawei Pay icon resource archive
published by Huawei Developers (resource page revision 2026/06/12):
https://developer.huawei.com/consumer/cn/design/resource/

These two files identify the notification's source application on the user's
paired watch. Their brand rights remain with Huawei. They are not covered by
the neighboring IconLibrary MIT license and do not imply Huawei endorsement.
Exact download URLs, archive entries and SHA-256 digests are recorded in catalog.json.

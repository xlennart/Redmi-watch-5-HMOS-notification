# Additional notification-source brand artwork

QQ Mail, WeRead, Tencent Meeting, ima and CamScanner images are unmodified PNG
application/site icons linked by the corresponding official websites or their
public application bundle. The source pages and exact download URLs are recorded
in catalog.json.

The ICBC, ABC, CCB, BOC and CMB brand symbols are from cellier/bank-icon-cn,
the bank symbol collection documented as used by Ping++:
https://github.com/cellier/bank-icon-cn
The exact Git blob identities are verified before inclusion and retained in
catalog.json. These are bank brand symbols, not a claim to reproduce every
platform's current mobile banking launcher artwork.

All artwork identifies the source of a notification on the user's paired watch.
Brand rights remain with their respective owners; inclusion does not imply
endorsement. These files are not covered by the neighboring IconLibrary MIT license.

Additional app artwork is taken from matching public Xiaomi App Store entries.
The store's application title is verified before downloading its PNG icon;
catalog.json retains the title, source page, image URL and digest. These are
publisher brand icons for the same application across platforms, not a claim
that Android store artwork always exactly matches the current HarmonyOS launcher.
